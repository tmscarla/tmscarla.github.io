Darione BBQ 🔥

Grill & Chill


Darione BBQ is a group of longtime friends who turned their passion for grilling and spending time together into something special. We wanted to create a barbecue done properly, where music, entertainment, and togetherness come together in an unforgettable experience. Darione BBQ cookouts aren’t just parties—they’re gatherings of one big extended family. Every time, the smoke from the grill blends with laughter, toasts, and refined music, creating a kind of magic that you can only understand by being there. We grill everything, from classics like sausages and burgers to our beloved lampredotto, with traditional preparations and a unique philosophy that even led us to take part as guests on the thirteenth season of MasterChef Italy!
